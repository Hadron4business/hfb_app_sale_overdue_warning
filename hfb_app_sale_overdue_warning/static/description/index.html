Customer Overdue Warning

See a customer's overdue invoices right on the quotation, block the order if you want, and send a statement in one click.

The problem

Odoo's credit limit looks at the total amount due, not at invoices that are already late. Salespeople keep confirming orders for customers who have not paid for months, and the accountant finds out too late.

What you get

Warning on the quotation

A banner shows the customer's overdue amount, with shortcuts to the late invoices and to the statement.

Tolerance in days

Invoices only a few days late are ignored. Set a company default and override it per customer.

Optional block

Block the confirmation of orders for customers with overdue invoices. Sales managers can still confirm - it is logged.

Overdue Statement

Printable PDF with every late invoice: dates, days overdue, amount due and total.

Send by e-mail

One click on the customer sends the statement with a ready-to-edit e-mail template.

Accurate amounts

Computed from open receivable items, so partial payments and credit notes are taken into account.

How to use it

1
Go to Sales > Configuration > Settings, section Quotations & Orders: set the Overdue Tolerance (days) and, if you want, tick Block Orders of Customers with Overdue Invoices.
2
Optionally, on a customer (tab Sales & Purchase, section Misc), set a different tolerance.
3
Open a quotation for a customer with late invoices: a yellow banner shows the overdue amount, with View and Print Statement.
4
On the customer form, the red Overdue button lists the late invoices and Send Overdue Statement e-mails the PDF.

Good to know

  • Amounts are in the company currency and per company.
  • Overdue = open receivable items whose due date is before today minus the tolerance.
  • Without the block option, orders can always be confirmed; the confirmation is noted in the chatter.
